NORTHSTAR COMPENSATION AND SALARY REVIEW POLICY
Policy ID: NS-HR-POL-004
Owner: Human Resources
Classification: Confidential - HR only
Valid from: 2026-01-01

1. Purpose
This policy describes how Northstar sets base salaries, runs the annual salary review and pays performance bonuses. It applies to all employees with an employment contract in Croatia and Austria.

2. Salary bands
Every role is assigned to one of eight salary bands. Each band has a minimum, a midpoint and a maximum. New employees are normally hired between the minimum and the midpoint of the band.

3. Annual salary review
The annual salary review takes place in February. Salary increases become effective on 1 April. The total salary increase budget for 2027 is EUR 310,000, which is approximately 3.8 percent of the total base payroll. Managers receive their team budgets by 15 January.

4. Performance bonus
Employees with a performance rating of "exceeds expectations" are eligible for a bonus of up to 12 percent of annual base salary. Employees rated "meets expectations" are eligible for up to 6 percent. Bonuses are paid with the March payroll.

5. Promotions
Promotions outside the annual review require approval by the HR Director and the CFO. A promotion normally comes with a salary increase of 8 to 15 percent.

6. Confidentiality
Individual salary data may only be accessed by HR, the direct manager and the payroll team. Salary information must never be shared in public channels.
