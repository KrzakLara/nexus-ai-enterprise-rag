ANTI-BRIBERY, GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY POLICY
Policy ID: NS-CMP-POL-002
Owner: Compliance
Applies to: all Northstar employees and contractors
Valid from: 2026-01-01

1. Zero tolerance
Northstar does not tolerate bribery in any form. Employees must never offer, promise, give, request or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision.

2. Gifts
Employees may accept occasional gifts from business partners with a value of up to EUR 50 per gift. Gifts above EUR 50 must be politely declined or handed over to Compliance. Cash, vouchers and gift cards must never be accepted, regardless of the amount.

3. Hospitality
Business meals with partners are allowed if they are reasonable and connected to a legitimate business purpose. Hospitality with a value above EUR 150 per person must be approved in advance by the line manager. Invitations to events that include travel or overnight stays require approval by Compliance.

4. Public officials
Gifts and hospitality for public officials are not allowed without prior written approval by Compliance, even if the value is below EUR 50.

5. Gift register
All gifts with a value above EUR 25, whether accepted or declined, must be entered in the gift register within 5 working days.

6. Reporting concerns
Employees can report suspected bribery through the confidential whistleblowing channel. Reports can be made anonymously. Northstar does not allow retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
