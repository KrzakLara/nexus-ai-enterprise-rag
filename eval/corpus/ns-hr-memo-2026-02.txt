CONFIDENTIAL MEMO - PROJECT KESTREL-9
Memo ID: NS-HR-MEMO-2026-02
From: HR Director
To: Management Board
Date: 2026-10-05
Classification: Strictly confidential - HR only

1. Subject
This memo describes the planned reorganisation of the Northstar customer service function under the code name KESTREL-9. The information must not be shared outside the HR leadership team and the Management Board until the official announcement.

2. Planned changes
The customer service teams in Zagreb and Graz will be merged into one shared service centre in Zagreb by 2027-03-01. The plan affects 23 positions in Graz. Northstar aims to offer internal transfers to at least 15 affected employees.

3. Severance
Employees who do not accept an internal transfer receive a severance payment of two monthly salaries per year of service, with a minimum of three monthly salaries. The estimated total severance cost is EUR 640,000.

4. Timeline
The works council in Graz will be informed on 2026-11-10. Individual meetings with affected employees take place between 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-28. The official internal announcement is planned for 2026-11-12.

5. Communication rules
Until the announcement, the code name KESTREL-9 must be used in all written communication. Documents related to the project are stored only in the restricted HR folder.

6. Risks
The main risks are loss of service quality during the transition and the departure of experienced employees before the merger is complete.
