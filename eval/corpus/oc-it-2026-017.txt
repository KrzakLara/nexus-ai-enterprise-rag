CYGNUS CLOUD SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT
Agreement ID: OC-IT-2026-017
Parties: Otherco Ltd (Customer) and Cygnus Cloud Services Ltd (Provider)
Service start: 2026-02-01
Term ends: 2028-01-31

1. Covered services
The Provider hosts the Otherco web shop and the order management system in its Vienna region.

2. Availability commitment
The Provider commits to a monthly availability of 99.5 percent for production services, excluding announced maintenance windows of up to eight hours per month.

3. Service credits
If monthly availability falls below 99.5 percent, the Customer receives a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly fee. If availability falls below 98.0 percent, the credit increases to 15 percent of the monthly fee.

4. Support and incident response
Critical incidents must receive a first response within 60 minutes during business hours and within 2 hours outside business hours.

5. Disaster recovery
The recovery time objective for production services is 12 hours and the recovery point objective is 4 hours. Disaster recovery tests are performed once per year.

6. Fees and notice
The monthly service fee is EUR 2,800. Either party may terminate the agreement with 90 days written notice before the end of the term.
