ACCESS CONTROL AND PASSWORD POLICY
Policy ID: NS-IT-POL-010
Owner: IT Security
Valid from: 2026-02-01

1. Accounts
Every user receives a personal account. Shared accounts are not allowed, except for documented technical service accounts that are owned by a named IT administrator.

2. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords do not expire on a fixed schedule but must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected. The last 10 passwords cannot be reused. Password managers approved by IT Security may be used.

3. Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for remote access, email, the ERP system and all administrator accounts. Hardware security keys are required for domain administrators.

4. Account lockout
An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. Repeated lockouts are reviewed by the security operations team.

5. Access reviews
Managers review the access rights of their team members every 6 months. Access to systems containing confidential HR or financial data is reviewed every 3 months. Accounts of employees who leave the company are disabled on their last working day.

6. Privileged access
Administrator rights are granted only for the time needed to complete a task, with a maximum of 8 hours per request. All privileged sessions are recorded.
