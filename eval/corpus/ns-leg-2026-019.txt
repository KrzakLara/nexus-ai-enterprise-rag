CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT
Agreement ID: NS-LEG-2026-019
Parties: Northstar Inc. (Client) and Helix Consulting GmbH (Consultant)
Effective date: 2026-06-01
End date: 2027-05-31

Note: Helix Consulting GmbH is a different company from Helios Analytics Ltd, with whom Northstar has a separate non-disclosure agreement.

1. Services
The Consultant supports the Northstar finance transformation programme, including the redesign of the month-end closing process and the selection of a new consolidation tool.

2. Fees
Services are charged on a time and materials basis at a daily rate of EUR 1,250 per consultant. The total fees under this agreement must not exceed EUR 240,000 without a written change request approved by the CFO. Travel expenses are reimbursed at cost, up to 8 percent of the fees.

3. Deliverables
The Consultant delivers a target process design by 2026-09-30 and a tool selection report by 2026-12-15. Each deliverable is accepted in writing by the Northstar programme manager within 10 business days.

4. Personnel
The Consultant assigns a lead consultant and up to three additional consultants. Replacing the lead consultant requires Northstar's prior written consent.

5. Termination
Northstar may terminate this agreement for convenience with 30 days written notice. In that case only services performed until the termination date are paid.

6. Intellectual property and law
All deliverables become the property of Northstar after payment. The agreement is governed by German law.
