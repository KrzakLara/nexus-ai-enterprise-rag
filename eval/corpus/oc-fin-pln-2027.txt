OTHERCO FINANCE STRATEGY 2027 - CONFIDENTIAL
Document ID: OC-FIN-PLN-2027
Code name: SILVER-IBIS-73
Prepared by: Otherco Finance
Date: 2026-10-01

1. Purpose
This document summarises the Otherco financial plan for 2027 under the code name SILVER-IBIS-73. It is intended only for the Otherco finance team and board.

2. Revenue and margin targets
Otherco targets revenue of EUR 31 million in 2027 and an EBITDA margin of 14 percent. The web shop is expected to contribute 35 percent of total revenue.

3. Capital expenditure
The capital expenditure budget for 2027 is EUR 2.2 million. The largest item is a new automated warehouse in Maribor with an estimated cost of EUR 1.4 million.

4. Cost programme
Otherco plans to reduce purchased services by 6 percent through supplier consolidation. Energy costs are expected to remain flat because of a fixed-price contract until 2028.

5. Financing
Capital expenditure is financed entirely from operating cash flow. No new bank loans are planned for 2027.

6. Confidentiality
This plan must not be shared with external parties, including suppliers and banks, before board approval on 2026-12-08.
