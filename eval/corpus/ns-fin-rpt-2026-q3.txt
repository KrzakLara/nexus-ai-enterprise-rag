NORTHSTAR QUARTERLY FINANCIAL REPORT - Q3 2026
Report ID: NS-FIN-RPT-2026-Q3
Prepared by: Group Controlling
Period: 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026

1. Summary
Revenue in the third quarter reached EUR 14.2 million, an increase of 6.1 percent compared with Q3 2025. Growth came mainly from the industrial components business in Austria. EBITDA was EUR 2.3 million, which corresponds to an EBITDA margin of 16.2 percent.

2. Costs
Operating costs were EUR 11.9 million. Energy costs decreased by 9 percent compared with the previous quarter after the new electricity contract started in July. Personnel costs increased by 4.5 percent because of new hires in the Graz plant.

3. Cash and working capital
Free cash flow for the quarter was EUR 1.1 million. Days sales outstanding improved from 58 days to 52 days. Inventory increased by EUR 0.8 million to prepare for the planned production ramp-up in Q4.

4. Capital expenditure
Capital expenditure in Q3 was EUR 0.9 million, mainly for the second painting line in Graz. Total capital expenditure for the first nine months of 2026 was EUR 2.6 million against a full-year budget of EUR 3.5 million.

5. Outlook
Group Controlling expects full-year 2026 revenue between EUR 55 million and EUR 57 million. The main risks are steel prices, the exchange rate of the Swiss franc for imported tools, and possible delays in the Graz ramp-up.

6. Distribution
This report is intended for the Finance team and the Management Board. Figures are unaudited.
