ATLAS INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Agreement ID: OC-SUP-2026-041
Parties: Otherco Ltd (Buyer) and Atlas Industrial Components GmbH (Supplier)
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Initial term ends: 2027-02-28

1. Scope of supply
Atlas Industrial Components supplies precision steel fasteners and hydraulic seals for the Otherco plant in Maribor. Orders are placed by email and confirmed by the Supplier within three business days.

2. Commercial terms
The estimated annual contract value is EUR 121,000. Prices are fixed for the full initial term. Invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt of a correct invoice.

3. Delivery and service levels
Standard delivery time is 15 business days from order confirmation. If the Supplier misses a confirmed delivery date, Otherco may claim a late delivery penalty of 1 percent of the order value per day of delay, up to a maximum of 8 percent of the order value.

4. Term, renewal and termination
The agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the current term. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 14 days of written notice.

5. Liability
Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to 100 percent of the annual contract value, except for damage caused by gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

6. Governing law
This agreement is governed by Slovenian law. Disputes are resolved by the district court in Maribor.
