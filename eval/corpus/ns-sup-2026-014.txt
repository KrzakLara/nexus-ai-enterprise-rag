ATLAS LOGISTICS FREIGHT SERVICES AGREEMENT
Agreement ID: NS-SUP-2026-014
Parties: Northstar Inc. (Customer) and Atlas Logistics d.o.o. (Carrier)
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Initial term ends: 2028-01-31

Note: Atlas Logistics d.o.o. is not related to Atlas Industrial Components GmbH. The companies only share a similar trade name.

1. Services
Atlas Logistics provides road freight between the Northstar plants in Zagreb, Graz and Ljubljana, including full truckload and less-than-truckload shipments. Temperature-controlled transport is not included in this agreement.

2. Prices
Freight is charged per route according to the rate card in Annex A. The estimated annual contract value is EUR 96,500. A fuel surcharge is applied monthly when the diesel price published by the national authority exceeds EUR 1.60 per litre.

3. Transit times and claims
The standard transit time between Zagreb and Graz is 24 hours. Damaged goods must be reported within 7 days of delivery. The Carrier's liability for lost or damaged goods is limited to EUR 25 per kilogram of gross weight.

4. Term and termination
The agreement has an initial term of two years. It renews for one-year periods unless either party gives written notice at least 120 days before the end of the current term. Northstar may terminate with 30 days notice if the on-time delivery rate falls below 92 percent in two consecutive quarters.

5. Invoicing
The Carrier invoices monthly. Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date. Disputed invoice lines must be raised within 15 days.

6. Governing law
This agreement is governed by Croatian law. Disputes are resolved by the commercial court in Zagreb.
