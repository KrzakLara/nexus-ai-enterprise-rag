AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO THE ATLAS INDUSTRIAL SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Amendment ID: NS-SUP-2026-041-A1
Amends: Agreement NS-SUP-2026-041 between Northstar Inc. and Atlas Industrial Components GmbH
Amendment effective date: 2026-09-01

Background
During the mid-year supplier review, Northstar procurement and Atlas Industrial Components agreed to shorten the renewal notice period and to extend the supplied product range. All provisions of the original agreement that are not changed by this amendment remain in force.

Change 1: Notice of non-renewal
Section 4 of the original agreement is replaced as follows. The agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term. The 90-day notice period in the original agreement no longer applies from the amendment effective date.

Change 2: Additional products
From 2026-10-01 the Supplier also delivers stainless steel couplings for the Graz paint shop. The estimated additional annual value is EUR 26,000, which raises the total estimated annual contract value to EUR 210,000.

Change 3: Urgent deliveries
Urgent line-stop critical orders must now be delivered within 36 hours instead of 48 hours. The late delivery penalty remains 0.5 percent of the order value per day of delay.

Change 4: Contact persons
Operational contact at Northstar: Supply Chain Desk, Zagreb plant. Operational contact at the Supplier: Key Account Team Central Europe.

Signatures
Signed for Northstar Inc. by the Head of Procurement and for Atlas Industrial Components GmbH by the Managing Director. This amendment is executed in two counterparts.
