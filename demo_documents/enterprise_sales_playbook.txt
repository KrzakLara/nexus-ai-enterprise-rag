NORTHSTAR SALES PLAYBOOK 2026
Document ID: NS-SALES-2026-012
Classification: Internal - Sales

The preferred enterprise sales cycle has five stages: qualification, discovery,
solution workshop, proposal, and procurement. The target response time for an
enterprise inbound lead is four business hours.

For contracts above EUR 100,000 annual value, sales directors must obtain approval
from the pricing committee. Standard discounts may not exceed 12 percent.
