Title: Employee Compensation Policy
Classification: Restricted - HR only

Salary bands and performance reviews are handled by the HR department. The annual
salary review occurs in February. Annual performance bonuses are approved by the
compensation committee and must not be disclosed outside authorized HR staff.

The budget allocated for salary increases in 2027 is EUR 310,000. Individual
compensation data must never be published in general company knowledge search.
